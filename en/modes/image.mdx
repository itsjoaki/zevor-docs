Image mode generates vertical 9:16 images from a prompt, or edits from
reference images.

## Variants

| Variant | What you bring | For |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Text to image** | A prompt | Create an image from scratch |
| **Image to image** | 1–3 references + prompt | Reinterpret or combine images |

## Quality

- **Lite** — fast and cheap for iterating.
- **Standard** — balanced for most cases.
- **Pro** — max fidelity and prompt adherence.

The credit cost depends on quality and is shown before you generate.

<Note>
  Each input image is capped at 5 MB. If you upload something larger,
  Zevor warns you so you can shrink it first.
</Note>

## Tips

- References win: to keep a face or a style, attach a clear image of it.
- Describe framing and lighting, not just the content.
