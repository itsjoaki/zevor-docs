Image mode generates vertical 9:16 images from a prompt, or edits
from reference images.

## Variants

| Variant | What you bring | For |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Text to image** | A prompt | Create an image from scratch. |
| **Image to image** | 1 to 3 references + prompt | Reinterpret or combine images. |

## Quality

- **Lite**: fast and cheap for iterating.
- **Standard**: balanced for most cases.
- **Pro**: max fidelity and best identity preservation when there are
  references.

## Cost estimate

| Quality | Cost per image |
| --- | --- |
| **Lite** | 2 cr |
| **Standard** | 4 cr |
| **Pro** | 6 cr |

Example: 4 Pro candidates = 24 cr. Fits ~5 series in a Starter month.

<Note>
  Each input image is capped at 5 MB. If you upload something larger,
  Zevor warns you so you can shrink it first.
</Note>

## Prompt examples

- **Text to image**: "Editorial medium shot, brunette model with
  freckles leaning on a car on a Lisbon street at sunset, warm natural
  light, looking at camera."
- **Image to image**: upload a photo + prompt "same character on a
  Madrid rooftop, sunset, neutral streetwear".

## Tips

- References win: to keep a face or a style, attach a clear image of
  it.
- Describe framing and lighting, not just the content.
- To keep the same face across series, instead of repeating the
  prompt use a saved avatar (see [AI Avatars](/en/modes/characters)).
