Video mode generates vertical 9:16 clips with native sound.

## Variants

| Variant | What you bring | When to use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Text to video** | Just a prompt | You have an idea but no image |
| **Image to video** | An image + prompt | You want to animate a specific image |
| **Frames to video** | Start and end image | You control the clip's start and end |

## Quality

- **Lite** — cheapest, ideal for iterating to find the prompt.
- **Pro** — publish-ready quality.
- **4K** — max resolution for hero pieces.

The credit cost is shown on the **Generate** button before you confirm,
and varies with quality, duration and whether audio is on.

<Note>
  If you attach an image, Zevor keeps the quality you picked — it does
  not auto-upgrade. You control the cost.
</Note>

## Best practices

- Describe action and camera, not just the subject ("medium shot, she
  turns and looks at camera" beats "a girl").
- For image to video, use a sharp, well-framed image: the video
  inherits its flaws.
