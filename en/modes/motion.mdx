title: "Motion"
description: "Transfer the movement from your clip onto another image."
---

Motion copies the movement from a reference video onto a character
image. You record yourself moving or talking; the result is that same
movement with a different face/body.

## What you need

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Character image" icon="image">
    The image to animate (e.g. one of your AI Avatars).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Motion video" icon="video">
    A short clip (3–30 s) of you moving or talking.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload the character image">
    Into the **CHARACTER** slot. It can come from the
    [Characters](/en/modes/characters) mode.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your motion clip">
    Into the **MOTION VIDEO** slot. Movement is copied frame by frame.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate">
    The prompt is optional; the reference clip drives it. You get the
    result to download in under a minute.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The original audio from the reference clip is kept in the result.
  Record with good light and the face visible for a clean transfer.
</Note>

Want a step-by-step tutorial with examples? It's at
