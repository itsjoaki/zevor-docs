An AI Avatar is a consistent character: same face, distinctive marks
and body across generations. You create it once, save it to your
library, and reuse it in any mode.

## Create an avatar

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe the character">
    Use the attributes (chips) to build the description, or write your
    own free-text prompt.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate and pick">
    The AI generates 4 candidates. Keep the one you like best.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Name it and it's added to your avatar library to reuse. You can
    also **Download** the face directly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## "Prompt only" mode

By default, if you don't pick chips, Zevor adds a random scene,
outfit and pose for variety and realism. If you want your text to be
the only thing driving it, turn on **Prompt only**:

- Uses just your prompt plus the core photorealism (real skin, no
  CGI, 9:16 framing with no cropping).
- Adds no random scene, outfit or pose.
- Ignores the builder attributes.

<Note>
  In "Prompt only" mode you need to type text: a lone chip isn't
  enough because the builder is ignored.
</Note>

## Reuse

From the avatar page you can:

- **Remix**: same face, different pose, outfit or scene.
- **Animate**: take it into [Motion](/en/modes/motion) as the character.
- **Talking photo**: make it say whatever you want.
- **Download**: the face, or all variations as a ZIP.
