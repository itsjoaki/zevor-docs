---
title: "Credits and plans"
description: "How credit usage works in Zevor."
---

Zevor runs on **credits**. Each generation deducts credits based on the
mode, quality and duration. The exact cost is always shown on the
**Generate** button before you confirm.

## Free accounts

Free accounts are created with **no credits**. To generate video or
images you need a paid plan.

## Plans

There are several monthly plans with different credit allowances, plus
one-time credit packs if you only want to top up occasionally. Current
prices and amounts are always at
[zevor.ai/pricing](https://zevor.ai/pricing) — that's the source of
truth, we don't repeat it here so it can't go stale.

<Note>
  Switching plans is instant and you can cancel anytime. Credits from
  one-time packs don't expire with the monthly cycle.
</Note>

## Why credits and not "unlimited videos"

AI video generation has a real compute cost. The credit model means you
pay for what you use, and high quality stays available without
artificial "X videos a month" caps.

## Managing your subscription

From **Account** you can see your balance, change plan, or open the
billing portal to update your card or cancel.
