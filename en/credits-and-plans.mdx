Zevor runs on **credits**. Every generation debits credits depending
on mode, quality and duration. The exact cost always shows on the
**Generate** button before you confirm.

## Monthly plans

| Plan | Price | Credits / month |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Starter** | $4.99/mo · **$1.99 first month** | 120 cr |
| **Pro** | $19.99/mo | 600 cr |
| **Max** | $49.99/mo | 1400 cr |

Yearly: ~20 % off the monthly rate, full year's credits available
from day one.

Every plan includes:

- **No watermark** on results.
- **Commercial use included**: the content is yours to publish.
- **Cancel anytime**, plan changes are immediate.

<Note>
  Free accounts are created **without credits**. You need a plan or a
  top-up pack to generate video or image.
</Note>

## Top-up packs

For one-off refills without changing your plan. Pack credits **never
expire** with the monthly cycle:

| Pack | Price |
| --- | --- |
| 30 cr | $4.99 |
| 70 cr | $9.99 |
| 150 cr | $19.99 |
| 320 cr | $39.99 |
| 700 cr | $79.99 |

Packs are spent **after** the monthly credits, so the plan's monthly
allowance doesn't get "burned" while you still have pack credit.

## How debits work

Every account has two balances: **monthly** credits (renew with your
subscription) and **top-up pack** credits (don't expire). Generations
spend pack credits first, then monthly, so you don't lose pack credit
for not using it in time.

If a generation fails on our side, the credits are **refunded
automatically** to the pack balance (the longer-lasting one).

## Managing your subscription

From **Account** you can see your balance, open the billing portal to
update your card or cancel, and buy top-up packs without leaving the
app.

## Why credits and not "unlimited videos"

Generating AI video has a real compute cost. The credit model means
you pay for what you use, and high quality is available with no
artificial "X videos a month" cap.
