Remove a video's background straight in the browser. No green screen,
no hand-rotoscoping: upload the clip and AI segments the subject frame
by frame, keeping edges and hair clean.

## What it's for

- Pulling a creator off any background so you can drop them onto a
  different plate or a solid color.
- Isolating a product for ads or catalog reels.
- Cleaning shots filmed without a set: a phone camera is enough.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload the video">
    MP4 horizontal or vertical, up to **30 seconds**. Resolutions up to
    1080p hold their detail.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate">
    The cost shows on the **Generate** button before you confirm.
    Segmentation runs frame by frame on our servers, nothing to
    install.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download">
    You get a video with the background cut out, ready to drop into
    CapCut, Premiere, Resolve or any editor.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Best practices

- **Good subject/background contrast**: if subject and background share
  the same tone, the segmentation loses edge detail.
- **Steady camera**: the less the camera moves, the cleaner the cut
  (the subject itself can move freely).
- **Fine details in clothing**: lace, fringes or very light hair are
  the hardest cases. Check the result at 100% before downloading.

## Limits

- 30 seconds max per clip. For longer videos, cut them into chunks and
  process each one separately.
- MP4 only. If your source is another container, convert it first with
  the [Video compressor](https://zevor.ai/herramientas/comprimir-video).
