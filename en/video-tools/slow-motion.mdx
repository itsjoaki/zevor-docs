Slow motion turns a regular-speed clip into a smooth slow-mo plate.
AI interpolates intermediate frames between your originals, so you
don't see the jumps or judder you get when you just slow a clip down
in an editor.

## What it's for

- Turn a phone recording (that wasn't shot in slow-mo) into a
  cinematic plate.
- Highlight gestures, expressions or details that fly past at full
  speed (bird wings, splashes, transitions).
- Intros and B-roll for reels where the slow pace is the "wow".

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload your clip">
    MP4, any format (horizontal or vertical).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the factor">
    **2x** (half speed) or **4x** (quarter). 4x is very cinematic but
    the clip lasts twice as long as 2x.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate and download">
    Runs in ~20-30 seconds. Download the final MP4, ready to post.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Cost

| Source duration × factor | Approx cost |
| --- | --- |
| 4 s at 2x | 8 cr |
| 8 s at 2x | 16 cr |
| 4 s at 4x | 16 cr |
| 8 s at 4x | 32 cr |

Priced per second of the **source** (2 cr/s at 2x, 4 cr/s at 4x).
Higher factor costs more because we generate more frames.

## Best practices

- Start with **short** clips (3-6 s). 4x slow-mo on 6 s is already
  24 s of output, plenty for a B-roll plate.
- **Steady camera** and **well-lit subject** pay off: interpolation
  needs to see well to invent well.
- Pair with [Upscale video](/en/video-tools/upscale-video) if you
  start from low resolution and want slow-mo AND sharpness.

## Limits

- No audio: the result comes muted. Add music or foley in your editor.
- MP4 only.
