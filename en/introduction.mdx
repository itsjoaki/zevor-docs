Zevor is an AI video and image generation tool built for creators. You
describe what you want, pick a mode, and get a vertical 9:16 result
ready to post — usually in under a minute.

## What you can do

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="AI video" icon="clapperboard" href="/en/modes/video">
    Text to video and image to video with native sound.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Motion" icon="person-running" href="/en/modes/motion">
    Transfer the movement from your clip onto another image.
  </Card>
  <Card title="AI Avatars" icon="user" href="/en/modes/characters">
    Create a character once and reuse it across every video.
  </Card>
  <Card title="AI images" icon="image" href="/en/modes/image">
    Generate and edit images from text or references.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Who it's for

Creators who post short-form daily and need volume without setting up a
shoot. It's not a video editor — it's the machine that produces the
footage you then upload.
