Zevor is an AI video and image generation tool built for creators.
You describe what you want, pick a mode, and get a vertical 9:16
result ready to post, usually in under a minute.

Text, image, motion-control and image generation: one product, four
paths from idea to video. No watermark, commercial use included.

## What makes it different

- **Consistent character**: the same AI avatar across every video,
  not a different face every time.
- **Spanish-first**: product and support built for Spanish-speaking
  creators (English available too), not a translation afterthought.
- **Browser-based**: nothing to install, desktop or mobile. Your
  account opens from any device.
- **No watermark, commercial use included** on every plan.

## Who it's for

Creators who post short-form daily and need volume without setting up
a shoot. It's not a video editor: it's the machine that produces the
footage you then upload.
