An AI influencer is an AI character that keeps **the same face** across
many videos and posts. The recipe: generate the face once, save it as
an avatar, reuse it on every generation.

## Outcome

- 1 saved avatar (fixed face).
- 3-5 photos of the avatar in different scenes (outfits, locations).
- 1-2 vertical 9:16 videos ready for TikTok or Reels.

## Time and credits

- **Total time**: ~30-45 minutes.
- **Credits**: ~80-120 cr.

Fits in a **Starter** month (120 cr), or a $4.99 top-up pack (30 cr)
if you already have the avatar created.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Generate the base face">
    In **Create → Characters**, describe the character with the chips
    (ethnicity, age, hair, distinctive marks) or write your own free
    prompt. Generate 4 candidates and keep the one you like best.
    
    Cost: 4 cr × 4 candidates ≈ 16 cr.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save as avatar">
    On the result card, **Save** + name. It lands in your avatar
    library and is reusable in any mode.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Variations (Remix)">
    From the avatar's card, **Remix**: change outfit, scene, pose.
    Three variations are plenty for a first series. The face stays.
    
    Cost: ~12 cr per 4-image variation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take the avatar to video">
    Two paths:
    - **Image to video**: use one of the variations as the base image
      and describe the motion (8 s, Pro with audio = 64 cr).
    - **Motion**: film a clip of yourself moving, upload the avatar
      as the character image, and the avatar copies the movement
      (Motion standard 6 cr/s).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Talking photo (optional)">
    If you want the avatar to speak, take it to
    [Talking photo](/en/video-tools/talking-photo) with your audio or
    TTS. 4 cr per second of speech.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Best practices

- **One face, many contexts**. The strength of an AI influencer is
  that the audience recognizes the face across posts. Change the
  scene, not the identity.
- **Iterate on Lite**. Find the prompt on Lite, then bump up to Pro
  or 4K for the hero piece.
- **Front or 3/4 shot** for video and for Talking photo: the output
  is much cleaner.
- **Save the prompt** that worked (it's in your history). Saves time
  on future series.

## Common mistakes

- **Changing the face every time**: if you describe the character
  from scratch on every generation, it comes out different. Create
  **one** avatar and reuse it.
- **Overlong prompts**: a dense description can contradict itself.
  Short sentences, one detail per sentence.
