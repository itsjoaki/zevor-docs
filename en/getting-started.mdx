<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an account">
    Sign up at [zevor.ai](https://zevor.ai) with email or Google.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get credits">
    Generating uses credits. Free accounts start with none, so pick a
    plan at [zevor.ai/pricing](https://zevor.ai/pricing) to start
    generating. See [Credits and plans](/en/credits-and-plans).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a mode">
    In **Create**, choose the mode based on what you have: just an
    idea, a photo, a motion clip, etc. Each mode has its own page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe and generate">
    Write a clear prompt, set quality and format, and hit **Generate**.
    The result shows in the panel; past generations stay in your
    gallery.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download and post">
    Download the MP4 or image and upload to TikTok, Reels or Shorts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Prompt tips

- Be specific about scene, lighting and framing.
- One detail per sentence: the AI follows short sentences better.
- If a result almost works, reuse it (Re-run) and tweak the prompt
  instead of starting over.

<Note>
  Prompt quality matters more than the model. Before switching modes
  or quality, try rewriting the description.
</Note>
